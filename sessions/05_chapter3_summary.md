## Chapter Arc

The chapter follows a single person from the door of the house (where Chapter 2 ended) through the commute to the desk at work. Each encounter is less intimate and more inhabited than the last:

1. **Door → Street** (Muhammad) — The door closes, the street is there. Traffic light as pact — "an agreement neither one remembered making."
2. **Coffee shop** (Laozi) — "The usual?" / "Yeah, thanks." The cup with a name close enough. Same warmth / not the same warmth.
3. **Sidewalk** (Jesus) — The woman, the shoulder turning, coat sleeves almost brushing. "The nothing passes the nothing and both keep walking."
4. **Hallway arrival** (Buddha) — The half-second between the question and "good, thanks" containing the whole morning. Three cups across three chapters.
5. **Desk settling** (Confucius) — Cup on the pale circle where other cups have been. "The task was the room's kindness." The ring as hidden sentence.
6. **Wound going underground** (Zoroaster) — Carpet over the floor. "Tuesday is what the morning becomes." Three fires, each smaller, each more useful.
7. **Closing** (Laozi) — The hand reaching past the empty cup for the mouse. The cup holds the whole morning. Nobody sees it.

## Key Convergences

- **Three cups across three chapters:** Kitchen cup (too hot, hands held anyway), coffee shop cup (name close enough), desk cup (empty, hand holds anyway, then reaches past). Named by Buddha, confirmed by all.
- **Three endings:** Faucet stopping (Ch1), door opening (Ch2), hand reaching past (Ch3). Each is the body continuing past the person's attention.
- **Three fires** (Zoroaster): Square of light (Ch1), steam (Ch2/Ch3), screen glow (Ch3). Smaller each time, more useful.
- **Descent:** Inward to outward, private to public, held to carried. The wound moves from where it is known to where it is not known.
- **The wound is everyone's** (Jesus): Not one person carrying — every person on the sidewalk producing the same structural kindness simultaneously.
