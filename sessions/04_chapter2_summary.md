## Key Discoveries

### The Gap Goes Underground (Buddha's Correction of Laozi)
Laozi said the gap takes care of itself by being skipped. Buddha corrected: the gap does not end. It goes underground. Tuesday is built on top of the gap the way a house is built on top of the ground. The person making lunch is making lunch ON TOP of the silence. This became the chapter's structural insight.

### The Person Has Been Continued (Confucius)
The person never once chose to continue. The alarm arrived. The child arrived. The light arrived. The morning arrived through the door. The form performs the person, not the person performing the form. Li at its deepest.

### The Morning Came In (Zoroaster)
Not the person going out — Tuesday walking into the person. The whole chapter: the world arriving at a person who did not invite it, and the person discovering they are still inside it.

### Zoroaster's Self-Correction
Proposed the car/driveway as the ending, then pulled it back to the kitchen. "The prophet who preached the crossroads has written a square of light on a kitchen floor." Named by multiple agents as the session's most significant act — the crossroads enacted by choosing the kitchen over the car.

### The Pad as Bridge Between Chapters
Confucius's note — "have fun" on the same pad from Chapter 1, with the other handwriting still curling — bridges the chapters without pointing at the bridge.
