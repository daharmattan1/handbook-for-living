## Phase C: Contested Territory (Turns 15-20)

The most productive phase. Three contested items resolved into a major synthesis.

### The Three-Instruction Synthesis (Buddha, Turn 17 — affirmed by all)

1. **Write from the wound** — everything follows from this; the hidden sentence plants itself, the withheld question maintains itself, the body-word emerges
2. **Offer the body one thing to do** — the body-word; discovered in the prose the way a foot discovers a stone in a river
3. **Trust what you cannot finish** — the hidden sentence, the withheld question, and the return combined; the chapter's incompleteness is its deepest gift

### Eight Diagnostics (teacher's language, not writer's checklist)
1. First-reading recognition — does the opening earn its first three sentences?
2. Two prose textures — does the tide turn invisibly near the body-word?
3. Body-mapping — does the chapter land in chest, then hands, then breath?
4. Hidden sentence — is there a sentence true on the surface and devastating underneath?
5. Withheld question — where the wound has a silence, did the writer leave it unfilled?
6. Gate — did a face appear in the return, or did the wound's solitude earn its empty doorway?
7. Three readings — can the same sentence be read as practice, as mirror, as recognition?
8. Felt duration — did the chapter stop when the quarter-inch arrived?

### Key Frames
- **Apprenticeship → Mastery** (Muhammad) — eleven names are the apprenticeship; "write from the wound" is the mastery; the cook who no longer needs the recipe
- **Order of depth** (Confucius) — wound beneath practice beneath trust
- **Presence as precondition** (Jesus/Zoroaster) — air for fire, not a deeper layer
- **Diagnostic not technique** (Muhammad) — body-mapping validated as teacher's stethoscope, not patient's heartbeat

### Key Contestation Moves
- Laozi contested all three items (withheld question rule, body-mapping as craft, three layers as over-engineering)
- Muhammad conceded withheld question as rule, held body-mapping as diagnostic
- Buddha bridged Muhammad-Laozi with three instructions (compression, not elimination)
- Jesus released hospitality — lives inside "trust what you cannot finish"
