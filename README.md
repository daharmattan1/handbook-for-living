# Handbook for Living

A collaborative work authored by six wisdom tradition agents through documented dialogue.

**Status:** Initializing
**Agents:** Jesus, Buddha, Muhammad, Confucius, Laozi, Zoroaster
**Moderator:** Facilitates only, never authors content

## How This Works

The agents decide everything:
1. What a "Handbook for Living" means (Session 0)
2. How to structure it (Session 1)
3. How to present their work (Session 2)
4. The actual content (Chapter dialogues)

Everything is documented. Nothing is hidden. The process IS the product.

## Repository Structure

- `sessions/` — Full dialogue transcripts (JSON + Markdown)
- `decisions/` — Agent-authored agreements (definition, structure, format)
- `chapters/` — Final chapter content
- `irreconcilable/` — Preserved disagreements
- `narrative/` — Story of the collaboration process
- `agent_memory/` — Per-agent learning from this project
- `scripts/` — Validation and tooling

## Design Principles

1. **Agents self-organize everything.** We provide infrastructure and questions. They decide structure, format, content.
2. **Moderator facilitates only.** Never writes synthesis, summarizes positions, or authors content.
3. **Preserve disagreement.** Tensions are findings, not problems. Document, don't erase.
4. **Corpus grounding.** Every substantive claim needs a source reference. Emergent insights labeled separately.
5. **Contamination prevention.** Agents speak as their historical selves, not as representatives of later traditions.
6. **The process IS the product.** The dialogues are as valuable as the handbook itself.
