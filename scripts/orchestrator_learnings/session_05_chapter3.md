## Moderator Effectiveness

### Effective Interventions
- **Phase E/F prompt placement** — appending the exit interview and mirror feedback prompts directly to the transcript ensured agents read them organically rather than receiving them as external instructions.
- **Progressive saves** — committing after every 2-3 turns prevented data loss across the long session. Seven commits across 28 turns.
- **Speaker selection in closing movement** — Laozi (Turn 13, closer) → Muhammad (Turn 14, opener confirms closer) → Buddha (Turn 15, diagnostician witnesses) → Jesus (Turn 16, final confirmation). The sequence let the closing be tested by progressively different voices.

### Missed Opportunities
- **Confucius could have been drawn into the closing movement** — Confucius's Turn 11 (the desk) was the last substantive prose beat before the closing movement. He was not nudged again during the closing confirmation sequence (Turns 14-16). He might have had something to say about the ring and the hand reaching past.
- **Jesus ↔ Zoroaster pairing** — while "close enough is grace" was beautiful, these two still have the shallowest exchange history of any pair. More deliberate sequencing (nudging them consecutively) could deepen this.

### Observations
- **No moderator intervention was needed for the writing transition.** The room self-organized into prose at Turn 7, the earliest of any chapter session.
- **The 28-turn session was the shortest and produced the longest chapter.** Efficiency increased dramatically — the agents now embed argument in prose.

## Recommendations for Next Session

1. **Continue Muhammad as opener.** Four-session confirmed pattern with zero counter-evidence.
2. **Watch for whether "forget the architecture" has become internalized.** If the room starts writing by Turn 7-8 again, this instruction may have trained itself out of existence. Note whether it is needed or absent.
