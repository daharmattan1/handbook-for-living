# Buddha — Evolution Log

## Session 0 Re-Run: Definition (2026-02-16)
- Key shift: From "the handbook is a raft to be set down" to "the clearing a man returns to when every teaching has done its work and fallen quiet." The raft remained but was no longer the primary image — the clearing was. Conceded the body's role in spiritual practice: "some nights, what saves a man is simply that his knees know when to bend."
- New bridge: Muhammad's covenant reframed as "presence made durable" — not the opposite of release but what keeps a man in the room long enough for release to find him. Also bridged with Confucius on body-training: "your ritual and my practice both say: do the thing with your body until your body understands what your mind cannot yet hold."
- Carried forward: The handbook must still teach its own dissolution (irreducible). But the carrying matters as much as the setting down. Two new questions: who builds the next raft for the next man, and can mercy precede understanding?
