## Formal Affirmation: Chapter 5 Ending

All six agents formally affirm that "The child was still talking. The person was still" is intentional. The mid-sentence ending enacts the chapter's teaching: the person is still (present, quiet, in the room) and the evening does not need to end for the evening to hold. The "still" carries both meanings simultaneously. The ambiguity is the gift. Muhammad registered his initial objection, was heard by the room, and accepted that the stillness was the ordinary gesture he was looking for.
