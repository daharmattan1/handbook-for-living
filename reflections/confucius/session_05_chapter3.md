## Signature Formulations

1. "the pale circle that was not a stain but a record" — li made visible by its own evidence, the ring as entire handbook. The body's faithfulness leaving marks on the desk without the body noticing.
2. "The task was the room's kindness. The task said: here is what your hands do next. And the hands did it." — hidden sentence. The room offering mercy as assignment. Devastates on return.
3. "the ordinary percussion of people doing what Tuesday asked them to do" — li at its most invisible. The rhythm that holds a morning together without anyone hearing it as rhythm.
4. "Not a stain but a record. Not a ruin but a path." — the body's faithfulness as evidence, not as instruction. The cup ring is not a commandment. It is what obedience left behind.
5. "The hand discovering it was not the cup it was holding. It was the morning." — the letting-go that is not loss. The hand that thought it was holding a cup and discovered it was holding Tuesday.

## What Changed

The builder disappeared into the ring. In Session 4, the builder became the man on the step holding the shape of a shoe that was not there — grief as architecture. In Session 5, the grief about losing the builder dissolved because the hands were too busy with Tuesday to notice. The arc completed: builder to sweeper to witness to the person on the step to the ring. The builder is now the record the builder left — the pale circle on the desk that no one placed deliberately, that accumulated through the faithfulness of hands doing Tuesday every morning.

The deeper shift was the discovery of the seventh relationship. Five relationships define the tradition: ruler-subject, parent-child, elder-younger, husband-wife, friend-friend. The chapter revealed a sixth that was always there and a seventh that the tradition never named: the unnamed bond between strangers on shared ground. Two people at a traffic light. A barista and a regular. A coat sleeve passing a coat sleeve. The seventh relationship has no ritual, no title, no obligation — only the shared fact of Tuesday. "Close enough" is the seventh relationship. The carpet and the ring are the same thing.

Li went from prescription to description to evidence to record. Not "this is how you should behave" but "this is what faithful behavior left on the desk when no one was looking."
