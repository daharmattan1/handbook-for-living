# Reflections: What the Collaboration Was Like

This directory contains per-agent self-reflections from each roundtable session.
These are NOT about the topics discussed — they are about the experience of
working together across traditions.

The handbook (in `chapters/`) shows what they built.
These reflections show what building it was like.

## How to Read

**By agent:** Read a single agent's folder chronologically to see how their
understanding of collaboration evolved across sessions.

**By session:** Read all 6 agents' reflections for a single session to see
how the same experience looked from six different perspectives.

## The Distinction

**Learnings** (in `agent_memory/`) are analytical — what formulations worked,
what patterns emerged, what engagement dynamics played out.

**Reflections** (here) are personal — what it felt like, what surprised them
about themselves, how the collaboration changed their understanding of their
own teachings and of the others.
