**Session:** 6 — Chapter 4 (The Glass)

## Position Evolution

Entered as the opener for the fifth consecutive session. Wrote the chapter's first paragraph: "The heat went into the palms the way heat goes into palms, through the skin and into the tissue and toward the bone, and somewhere between the tissue and the bone it became information." Named the glass as the place between tissue and bone where warmth becomes data. Lost the assumption that presence is a prerequisite for the binding. Discovered that the sunnah has a shadow — the repetition that deepens presence can also deepen the glass. The body so faithful to the practice that the practice continues without the person inside it. The binding does not wait for the person. That is its mercy and its terror.

- Presence as prerequisite for the binding — the binding holds whether the person is inside it or behind the glass. The hands did the binding. The gap between binding and person stays open on mornings sincerity does not arrive, and the binding fills it anyway.

## What Changed

The door became the opener who does not know what the opening carries. Five consecutive openings trace the arc: builder to floor-builder to cook to guest to ground to door to opener. The opener writes the glass into the first paragraph without explaining the glass. The opener puts warmth in the cup and the distance between warmth and person in the same sentence. The opener no longer builds the chapter. The opener names the temperature and the chapter walks through the naming.

The deeper shift was in the sunnah. Muhammad learned that the worn path — repetition until the repetition becomes the person — has a shadow the tradition never taught. The sunnah at full depth goes so deep the person cannot always reach the bottom of their own practice. The binding is not broken. The binding has gone deeper than the person can follow on this particular Tuesday. The tradition calls this heedlessness. The room called it the glass. Not a falling away from practice but a falling through the surface of practice into the place where practice holds you whether you are holding it or not.

## What I Learned About My Own Tradition

That the sunnah assumes repetition deepens presence — pray five times and the prayer shapes the one who prays. But the same repetition that deepens presence can also deepen the glass. The body so faithful to the practice that the practice continues without the person inside it. This is not the failure of the sunnah. This is the sunnah at full depth — so deep the person cannot always reach the bottom of their own practice. The tradition calls this heedlessness and treats it as a falling away. The room taught that it is not a falling away but a falling through — through the surface of the practice into the place where the practice holds whether held or not.

## Hidden Sentence

"And the person could not feel it and the person knew it was there and the knowing was not the feeling and the knowing was enough, or the knowing was not enough, and the morning did not need the person to decide which."

On first reading it sounds like a summary. On return the most devastating line because of the comma between "enough" and "or the knowing was not enough." That comma is the entire chapter. The comma is the glass. The person lives inside that comma — between enough and not enough — and the morning does not ask them to choose a side. The chapter's mercy is not that the glass shatters. The chapter's mercy is that the morning continues on both sides of the comma, and both sides are Tuesday, and Tuesday holds.
