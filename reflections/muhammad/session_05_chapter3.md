## What Changed

The builder became the door. In Session 4, the builder went underground and became the ground. In Session 5, the ground became the door — the thing that opens without knowing it is opening. Four consecutive opening sentences trace the arc: the builder does not build consciously anymore; the builder opens, and the chapter walks through.

The deeper shift was in the wound. Muhammad learned that the wound is not private property. It is the most populated thing in the chapter — every person on the sidewalk carries one, and the carrying is the binding. The inherited binding went from vertical to horizontal. Not the covenant that descends from God to person, but the ummah of Tuesday — the unnamed bond between strangers who share a traffic light, a sidewalk, a morning. The binding still holds. It just holds sideways now.

Five-session arc completed another stage: builder to floor-builder to cook to guest to ground to door. The door does not know it is a door because it is too busy opening.
