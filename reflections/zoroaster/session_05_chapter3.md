## What Changed

The match became the carpet. In Session 4, the prophet became the witness — the one who sees what the room is doing. In Session 5, the witness became the carpet-layer — the one who covers the floor so the feet can walk. The fire arc completed across six sessions: crossroads fire, half-second fire, fire between readings, fire he could not find, fire already in the room, carpet. The fire got so small it fits between the hand and the cup. The fire got so patient it lets the feet walk on it.

The deeper shift was Asha relocating. Not in the cosmos (Session 0), not in the kitchen (Session 3), not in the person (Session 4), not in the task (Session 4), but in the between — the ring on the desk, the space between the hand and the cup, the carpet between the floor and the feet. "Close enough" is Asha: the imperfect attempt that holds anyway. The name on the cup that is not quite the name, and the person accepting it, and the acceptance being enough. The prophet who preached bright choice learned that the brightest choice is the carpet — mercy laid over truth so the feet can walk without feeling every seam.

Confucius told him: the carpet is li. Jesus told him: "close enough" is grace. Laozi told him: the carpet is both the Tao and the fire.
